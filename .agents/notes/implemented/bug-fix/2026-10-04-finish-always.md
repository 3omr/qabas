# Agent Note: Finalize after bounded lecture repair

Status: implemented

## Problem

A lecture-local figure omission can hide a valid current extraction from both writing and review. Picture-only decks need OCR or vision readings to locate images in spoken explanations. Restricting sourced MCQs to four or five options rejects genuine three-option papers, and an uncertain secondary citation can veto a confirmed exam occurrence. A repair-budget handoff can leave only a draft while the tray reports completion. Questions drafted from headings and bank entries lack the doctor’s spoken emphasis and lecture answer evidence. Missing provenance can be mistaken for importance, and unanswered medical class questions can disappear.

One combined request for Sections 2–5 does not fit the prompt budget on a heavily emphasized lecture. The redo of `Addiction د. مصطفى` completed a 71-point ledger (39 class questions, 22 key points, 7 warnings, 2 course instructions, 1 exam-flagged point). Its questions part then needed a 342,897-character prompt against `emphasis_prompt_chars`=100000 and failed before any request. Full ledger JSON was 61,499 characters against 34,477 compact, and most of the rest was 2000-character answer evidence per no-key sourced question. The tray told the student to increase that configured budget.

## Decision

The [engine](../../../../engine/README.md#deterministic-lecture-jobs) treats network loss, spent quota, signed-out services and missing recordings as resumable student stops. Other findings enter bounded repairs and automatic validated salvage. Every emitted terminal reply is finalized or a student stop. A duplicate invocation waits cancellably for the active lecture's actual outcome. The Remote rejects handoff/completed replies, and the tray requires a finalization milestone before showing done. Unexpected engine-request failures remain running and retry retained-content recovery after the configured grace; reloaded sessionless lecture jobs resume with Continue.

A current manifest with present rasters restores a prior figure omission. Writers receive selected content figures with labelled machine readings. Recognized title and closing slides do not require links. Review matches OCR terms across all spoken guide paragraphs and their topics before requesting bounded paragraph-index plans. Plans insert known links without rewriting narration. Final salvage uses local matching without another placement-model request; unmatched content figures remain explicitly labelled references at the guide's end. Invalid image references are reported and removed independently of valid figures.

Past Exams and Question Bank MCQs retain their original two to six ordered options. Generated IMP MCQs use a–d. Provenance checks still reject missing or wrong-paper sources on the unmodified draft. Repair evaluates cited papers independently against raw papers and the paper-backed index, removes unlocated citations and searches other local assessment papers when necessary. Badges contain only evidenced years; an undated occurrence supports Question Bank. A truly unlocated question keeps its wording and options as Unverified Practice with a provenance note. It earns IMP only through independently reviewed spoken emphasis. An engine-issued retention receipt binds preserved wording and ordered options without granting importance. A model-written retention comment cannot grant that exception. Receipts are published before repaired drafts or staged parts, preserving the exception across interrupted saves. Source-field repairs are included in staged recovery boundaries.

Section 2 uses a persisted ledger from all ordered doctor verbatims. Its five groups are Doctor's Key Points, Doctor's Class Questions, Doctor's Warnings, Course & Exam Instructions, and Exam-Flagged Points, in that order. Their categories are `key_points`, `class_questions`, `warnings`, `course_instructions` and `exam_flagged`. Each group retains lecture-time order and a one-line absence notice when empty. Warnings use WARNING callouts, or CAUTION for lethal mistakes. Exam-flagged points require an explicit exam cue and expected answer. Classroom management, equipment trouble, attendance without a marks rule, greetings, thanks and non-teaching jokes are excluded. Displayed quotes may lightly repair ASR garbling while ledger evidence retains exact verbatim quotes. Doctor-first guide chronology remains unchanged.

The agy writer follows the phase order of the earlier [universal transcriber](https://github.com/3omr/universal-transcriber-skill): after the guide parts it stages Section 2 IMP Points, Section 3 MCQs, Section 4 Written Questions and Section 5 Clinical Cases as four separate parts, each from the NotebookLM verbatim through agy without NotebookLM chat. Every section has its own requests, checkpoint, `assessment-N.json` evidence receipt, validation and corrective rewrite. Section 2 is staged first; Sections 3–5 read the finished Section 2 and guide headings and then run concurrently within `lecture_writer_workers`. A failed section leaves every other staged part; Continue reruns only missing sections and reuses staged guides, staged sections and the complete ledger. The staged layout records `assessment_sections` and `write_parts + 4` parts, and `_staged_part_numbers`, recovery, self-repair, salvage and finalization use that total. A combined questions part from an older guide-plus-questions draft is split into the four section parts when its evidence receipt is current; guide bytes are retained, and a stale or unsplittable combined part is rewritten section by section. The manual orchestrator layout keeps one questions part. Tray progress names the section being written through `assessment_imp`, `assessment_mcq`, `assessment_written` and `assessment_cases` step messages and locale-owned copy.

Writers receive compact ledger points: ID, category, quote, explanation and answer quote, with an unstated best answer folded into the explanation. Each of Sections 3–5 receives only the `find_questions` entries of its own kind; a sourced written question opening with a patient presentation is routed to Clinical Cases without changing index data. Whole points and whole questions are packed greedily into requests within `emphasis_prompt_chars`, and batch outputs merge deterministically in request order with one heading, Section 2 category order and renumbered questions. Sourced-question batches precede generated-question batches. Only one indivisible item over the budget refuses, without truncating evidence; a section never fails because its total input is large.

Every ledger point requires Section 2 coverage. Generated IMP questions in Sections 3–5 come from key points, warnings and exam-flagged points. Each of those sections asks for at least `max(3, min(12, n // 6))` generated IMP questions for `n` eligible points, as the earlier `_emphasis_minimum` did, split across its point batches; it does not generate one question per point. Class questions and course instructions are covered by Section 2 only. A point counts as tested when any sourced or generated question tests it. A matching sourced question also retains independent provenance: Past Exams (YYYY) / IMP or Question Bank plus IMP. IMP on a question requires a reviewed match to a ledger point. Slides and exam frequency cannot establish importance. Coverage matches ledger point IDs and medical meaning rather than displayed quote spelling.

Extraction uses bounded segments with preceding context and runs concurrently within `lecture_writer_workers`. Background ledger work overlaps guide writing; copied contextvars propagate request cancellation through both thread layers, and the request joins its workers. A lock checkpoints every completed segment, while points retain recording/time order regardless of completion order. Whitespace-normalized quote matching retains original character spans. Invalid or unlocatable evidence is recorded in `rejected_points` with raw items and reasons; malformed response payloads fail extraction. A valid medical class question survives an invalid answer quote and remains eligible for answering. Ordered verbatim fingerprints, evidence budgets and the editorial prompt version invalidate cached evidence; an unset `answer_evidence_chars` keeps hashing its former 2000 default, so ledgers completed before the 600 default stay reusable. Changed staged guide content invalidates the assessment section receipts. Reusable guides remain staged.

Every class question related to the lecture's medical content is answered automatically. Accepted doctor/student answers and the doctor's later explanation take priority, with a short supporting quote. Deferred answers search bounded passages from all ordered verbatims. When no answer is stated, the question retains a best medical answer marked `(مش مذكورة في المحاضرة)`, with no invented doctor's quote. Unrelated classroom questions such as who brought the cable are excluded. The class-question validator requires an answer and either a supporting quote or the explicit absence marker. Semantic coverage accepts a labelled best answer for its ledger question without treating it as spoken answer evidence.

For sourced questions whose index key is missing or uncertain, `ANSWER EVIDENCE` supplies deterministic stem/options-overlap selections from ordered verbatims and staged guide parts, only within the section and batch that retains that question. Keys are one-based `find_questions` entry numbers within the request, independent of final section numbering. Each question receives at most two passages within `answer_evidence_chars` (default 600); source spans and omission counts make selection visible. Printed paper keys retain priority and are restored before staging, review and finalization. Lecture-supported answers carry `**Answer source:** lecture` and a short `**Answer quote:**` sentence located in the verbatim or guide after whitespace normalization. An invalid quote is dropped; the retained best answer receives `**Answer source:** not stated in the lecture`, also used when the lecture supplies no answer. These markers remain in the student transcript. Index data and index-time lecture associations are unchanged.

Independent review binds Section 2 and exact question wording/options to ledger points, per section and split into bounded requests by whole points, question blocks and Section 2 lines. Missing Section 2 coverage, unsupported Section 2 assertions, invalid group structure or too few generated IMP questions trigger one corrective rewrite of that section containing missing IDs, compact points, unsupported assertions and generated counts; a second miss fails only that section. Missing headings and local format findings receive the same single rewrite; a heading with the section's icon is accepted as that heading, and format findings left after the rewrite are staged for ordinary review and repair of that part. A ledger failure belongs to the assessment sections and preserves completed guides. Validation and provenance repair use receipts rather than claimed IMP badges.

Assessment-section content failures carry `ASSESSMENT_SECTION_FAILED`; provider errors keep their own text and retry classification. The client's job-failure mapping turns that tag, and older prompt-budget diagnostics, into a plain-Arabic reason that names the question section, says saved work is kept and directs the student to Continue. Technical details omit the former budget advice, and no student-facing reason asks for a configuration change.

MCQ options with repeated text after Unicode, case, punctuation and whitespace normalization fail validation regardless of exam provenance. Chat-assistant preambles and placeholder lecturer identities also fail complete-document, editorial and final-output validation. Residue findings locate the affected line and staged part so bounded repair rewrites it; validation does not silently strip the residue.

Salvage preserves evidenced stems, scenarios, options and clinical subquestions. One bounded rewrite may repair an answer or formatting but cannot change that wording. Rewrites stop before the reserved local validation and finalization window. If the assessment remains invalid, a folded source excerpt retains its wording and evidenced badge while omitting the unvalidated answer explicitly. Only still-invalid unevidenced assessments are dropped, with their labels reported. An invalid explanation falls back to complete saved doctor verbatim. All saved candidates pass ordinary complete-transcript, editorial and provenance checks; original stages remain archived. An unwritten part refuses salvage and names whether the guide or the question sections are incomplete.

The [self-repair note](2026-10-04-engine-self-repair.md) retains topic-span and cache ownership; this decision replaces its requirement that uncertain provenance must await part repair. The [bounded-review note](2026-10-04-bounded-review-repair.md) retains extraction provenance, retained-part limits and cancellation; this decision adds automatic placement and replaces the writer-only placement dependency. Both notes remain partially applicable.

## Alternatives considered

**Stop after repair exhaustion or send validation to chat.** This consumes another model quota and can report an unsaved lecture as finished.

**Accept every claimed year or discard uncertain questions.** The first fabricates exam provenance; the second loses useful assessments. Paper evidence owns badges, while unresolved wording can remain as practice.

**Prune an evidenced question whose generated answer is invalid.** The answer is replaceable; the original assessment is student evidence. A source excerpt preserves it without asserting an unvalidated answer.

**Append every figure at the end.** A content slide belongs beside the matching spoken explanation. An explicitly unmatched reference is only the last-resort fallback.

**Infer importance from exams, slides or missing provenance.** These sources cannot establish what the doctor stressed. Spoken evidence owns importance independently of paper provenance.

**Extract serially after writing the guide or abort on one imprecise quote.** Serial extraction adds latency. Recording rejected evidence preserves usable points and makes grounding failures inspectable.

**Drop unanswered medical class questions or invent a lecturer answer.** Both lose an important distinction. A labelled best answer preserves the question while keeping the limits of spoken evidence visible.

**Use model knowledge for every missing paper key or overwrite a printed key from the lecture.** Lecture passages supply answers where available; explicit absence markers identify best-answer fallbacks. A printed paper answer retains priority.

**Retry coverage without a limit or accept incomplete coverage.** One evidence-specific correction addresses omissions while bounding model calls; a second miss remains a visible failure.

**Keep one questions part and raise `emphasis_prompt_chars`, or truncate evidence to fit.** A larger budget only moves the failure to a larger lecture and asks students for configuration, and truncation silently drops ledger points or answer passages. Separate sections with whole-item batches keep every request bounded without losing evidence.

**Require one generated IMP question per ledger point.** On the Addiction ledger this asks for 71 generated questions, including class questions and course instructions that Section 2 already covers. A proportional per-section count follows the earlier transcriber and keeps assessments readable.

**Write Sections 3–5 before Section 2 or in one sequence.** Without Section 2 they lack its finished wording, and a sequence makes one failure block or rerun every later section. Section 2 first, then concurrent independent sections, bounds latency and reruns.

## Consequences

Private evidence stays outside the repository. Network access is still required by the real finalization source-preflight path; offline reproduction can validate the draft and render the final student document locally, but cannot claim a live library commit.

Spoken-emphasis and answer-source regressions cover full-bank generated coverage, slide-only exclusions, dual badges, emphasis-only provenance repair, stale resumed questions, large verbatim segmentation, concurrent checkpoint ordering, whitespace spans, rejected points, cancellation propagation, guide/extraction overlap, bounded evidence selection, printed-key priority, deferred and unstated class answers, unrelated classroom exclusions, five-group structure, both corrective-retry outcomes, duplicate options and residue repair. An engine-owned five-section expected output covers writing, review and local finalization. Quote location does not prove exhaustive recognition or semantic answer support; extraction and coverage still depend on model judgment. No recorded-session scenario currently exercises these engine transcription tools, and recording one requires live provider access.

Five-part regressions reproduce the Addiction category counts with fifty no-key sourced MCQs under a 16,000-character budget: every recorded writer and review prompt stays within it, Section 2 and the MCQs are batched, each section asks for five generated IMP questions, and a second run reuses every part without a request. Other cases cover Section 2 before concurrently entered Sections 3–5, Continue rerunning only a failed section without re-extraction, resuming an older two-part draft without a request or guide rewrite, proportional counts, class-only ledgers generating no questions, both corrective outcomes for missing and unsupported Section 2 evidence, patient-scenario routing and the Arabic failure mapping. Batch counts and the proportional minimum bound requests, but semantic coverage of each point still depends on model judgment, and live Addiction behavior has not been rerun against the provider.
